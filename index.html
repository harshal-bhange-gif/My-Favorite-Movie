My Favorite Movie

Avengers: Endgame

Avengers: Endgame is a superhero movie released in 2019. It is one of the most popular movies in the world and the final part of the Avengers series.

Main Actors:

  • Robert Downey Jr. (Iron Man)
  • Chris Evans (Captain America)
  • Scarlett Johansson (Black Widow)
  • Chris Hemsworth (Thor)

Story:

The Avengers try to bring back the people who disappeared after Thanos snapped his fingers. They travel through time to collect infinity stones and defeat Thanos in a final battle.

Why I Like This Movie:

  1. Amazing action scenes
  2. Emotional story
  3. Strong teamwork
  4. Great ending

My Favorite Scene:

My favorite scene is the final battle where all superheroes come together through portals. Also, Iron Man’s snap scene is very emotional and powerful.
